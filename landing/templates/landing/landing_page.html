Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education.

Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to nation-building.

Core Values

Maka-Diyos — Faith in God as the foundation of moral character.
Makatao — Respect for human dignity and rights.
Makakalikasan — Care for the environment and sustainable living.
Makabansa — Love of country and commitment to national development.
